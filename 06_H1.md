## Phase 2: Coding, FLOW_BENCH.py, the read benchmark (about 2-3 hours)

The club compares machine reads against the chip with this script. Build it now:

1. Download the real model file: https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai/raw/main/src/model.bin . Save it as model.bin in your working folder.
2. Write a script, under 50 lines, stdlib only (time, os, statistics).
3. Read model.bin in 512-byte chunks. Time every read with time.perf_counter.
4. Do the whole read 10 times. Collect every chunk time.
5. Print: the file size, the chunk count, and for the chunk times: minimum, median, and maximum in microseconds.
6. One token on the chip needs 6 row reads. Print the projected tok/s for the median: 1,000,000 divided by (6 times the median in us).
7. Run it and keep the output.

Save it as FLOW_BENCH.py. Write the script yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool.

## Phase 3: Maths, memory budget (about 2 hours)

Show every step, with units:

1. Flash: 16 MB total. model.bin is 14.9 MB. Free flash: 16 minus 14.9, in MB, and as a percentage of 16 MB.
2. SRAM is 512 KB. Divide 14.9 MB by 0.5 MB. How many times bigger is the model than SRAM?
3. Working set estimate: assume the head is 1% of the model, about 149 KB. What percentage of PSRAM 8 MB is that?
4. One inference step reads 6 rows of 512 bytes. Working rows: 6 times 512 bytes, in KB. What percentage of SRAM 512 KB is that?
5. Write 2 sentences: where the table lives and why it cannot live in SRAM.
