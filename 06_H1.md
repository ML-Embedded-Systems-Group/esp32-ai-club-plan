# H1: ML + embedded, week 1

Member: ____________
Time budget: 5-8 hours across the week.

## What this week gives you

No code this week. No cloning. No accounts. You read, you take notes, and you draw one flow diagram. The project runs a small language model on a microcontroller. By Friday you can explain that idea in plain words.

## Part 1: Read and take notes (about 3-4 hours)

Read these in order:

1. What a language model is: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_model (first 2 sections)
2. What an ESP32 is: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESP32 (first 3 sections)
3. The project readme: https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai (read the description, skip the code)
4. The results page: https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai/blob/main/RESULTS.md (look at the numbers, skip the code)

Take notes as you read, at least 40 lines. Handwritten or typed, your choice. Write what each thing means in your own words. Do not copy-paste.

## Part 2: Your deliverable, from model to device (about 2-3 hours)

Write MODEL_TO_DEVICE.md.

1. Draw the flow, on paper or in the file: training, quantization, export to model.bin, model file stored in flash, runtime reads weights, chip generates text.
2. Under each stage, write what happens in one line.
3. Where a number exists in RESULTS.md, write it under the stage. Examples: file size, tok/s, memory free numbers.
4. Take a photo of the paper drawing if you drew it by hand, and add it to the file.

Rules: the flow must make sense to someone who never saw the repo. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.
