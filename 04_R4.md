## Phase 2: Maths, perplexity by hand (about 2 hours)

Show every step:

1. Uniform over 8 words. Sequence of 4 words. Probability of the sequence: 1/8 to the power 4. Compute the fraction.
2. Perplexity: the fraction to the power -1/4. Show the power step. The result must be 8. Verify by writing the fourth root of the inverse.
3. General proof: uniform over N words, sequence of n words. Show that ppl = N, using symbols, not numbers.
4. Set A: cat 0.5, dog 0.3, sun 0.2. Sequence: cat, sun, dog, cat. Multiply the 4 probabilities, then ppl = product to the power -1/4.
5. Set B: cat 0.4, dog 0.35, sun 0.25. Same sequence. Repeat.
6. Write 2 sentences: which set is more confident, and what the article means by "perplexity is the branching factor".
