## Part 2: Your artifact, compute perplexity (about 2-3 hours)

1. Make a tiny vocab of 3 words with probabilities, for example: cat 0.5, dog 0.3, sun 0.2.
2. Take a sequence of 4 words, for example: cat, sun, dog, cat. Compute the probability of the whole sequence, step by step.
3. Compute perplexity: ppl = probability raised to the power -1/4. Show every step. Round to 2 decimals.
4. Repeat with a second probability set, more even: cat 0.4, dog 0.35, sun 0.25. Same sequence. Which ppl is lower? Why?
5. Write one sentence: the results page reports ppl 12.58. What does that number mean?
6. Save everything as PPL_CALC.md and push it to this repo.

Rules: show every step of the math. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.
