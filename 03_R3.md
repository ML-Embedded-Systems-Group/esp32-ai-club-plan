## Part 2: Your deliverable, tokenization by hand (about 2-3 hours)

Write TOKENIZATION_BY_HAND.md.

1. Make a pretend vocab of 8 pieces. Use letters and short groups, for example: a, e, th, er, ing, space, ., ?
2. Tokenize 5 sentences with it. For each sentence: write the sentence, the sequence of pieces, and the piece count.
3. Now make the pretend vocab bigger: 16 pieces. Tokenize the same 5 sentences again.
4. Answer in writing: what changes between 8 and 16 pieces? The repo uses 32768 pieces. What does a bigger vocab give, and what does it cost?

Rules: do this on paper first, then type it up. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.
