## Phase 2: Maths (about 2 hours)

Problem: run the paper's BPE algorithm on the four words the paper uses: low, lower, newest, widest. Start from single characters, each word ending with the word boundary marker. Count every adjacent pair, merge the most frequent pair, and repeat. Show every merge with its count.

## Phase 3: Coding, BPE_REPRO.py (about 2-3 hours)

Reproduce the paper's algorithm and the real GPT-2 tokenizer:

1. Download the real GPT-2 tokenizer files:
   - https://huggingface.co/gpt2/resolve/main/merges.txt
   - https://huggingface.co/gpt2/resolve/main/vocab.json
   Save them in your working folder.
2. Write a script, under 90 lines, stdlib only.
3. Part 1: implement BPE. Run it on the four words from phase 2. Print the pair counts and the merge order. Your merge order must match your phase 2 list.
4. Part 2: load merges.txt and vocab.json. Encode one sentence of your choice. Print the pieces, their ids, and the token count.
5. Part 2b: tokenize the same sentence on https://tiktokenizer.vercel.app with GPT-2. Print its count. Compare with yours.
6. Print the round trip: decode your ids back to text and verify it equals the sentence.

Save it as BPE_REPRO.py. Write the script yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool.
