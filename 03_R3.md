## Part 2: Your artifact, tokenization hands-on (about 2-3 hours)

1. Open https://tiktokenizer.vercel.app. It tokenizes text with real tokenizers in your browser. No account needed.
2. Type 5 sentences into it. Record: the sentence, the token count, and the pieces for the GPT-2 tokenizer (vocab 50257).
3. Switch to the LLaMA tokenizer (vocab 32000). Record the same 5 sentences again.
4. Make a small bar chart of the token counts, GPT-2 vs LLaMA. Any tool, even a sheet or a drawing.
5. Do one byte-pair merge pass by hand on a short text of your choice, 5 to 8 words. Show 2 rounds: count the pairs, merge the most frequent pair, count again. This is how BPE works.
6. Write TOKENIZATION_HANDS_ON.md with the records, the chart, and the merge pass. Answer: the repo uses 32768 pieces. What does a bigger vocab give? What does it cost? Push it to this repo.

Rules: the merge pass must be step by step. Show every round. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.
