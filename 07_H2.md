## Part 2: Your deliverable, memory tiers (about 2-3 hours)

Write MEMORY_TIERS.md.

1. Explain the 3 memory tiers of the ESP32-S3: 512 KB SRAM, 8 MB PSRAM, 16 MB flash. One paragraph each. What is each one good for?
2. The model file is about 14.9 MB. Answer in writing: why does it not fit in SRAM? Where does it live instead?
3. The results page says the table of weights is about 25M values and the head is about 1.64 MB. Answer: why does the head live in PSRAM and the table in flash?
4. Explain the numbers: 294 KB SRAM free, 4.22 MB PSRAM free. Free after what?

Rules: plain words only. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.
