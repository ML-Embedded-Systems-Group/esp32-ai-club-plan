## Part 2: Your artifact, change the firmware (about 2-3 hours)

1. Open https://wokwi.com. Start a new ESP32 project, the blinking LED example. Press Play. Take a screenshot and save it as BEFORE.png.
2. Open the code editor. Change the blink delay from 1000 ms to 200 ms.
3. Press Play again. Take a screenshot and save it as AFTER.png.
4. Write 3 sentences: what changed in the code, what flashing means on a real board, and what the simulator skips.
5. Save both screenshots and the 3 sentences as FIRMWARE_EDIT.md. Push it to this repo.

Rules: your 3 sentences must be your own words. No copy-paste. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.

## Part 3: Meetup demo
