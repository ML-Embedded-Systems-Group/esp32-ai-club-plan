# E2: Embedded, week 1

Member: ____________
Time budget: 5-8 hours across the week.

## What this week gives you

No code this week. No flashing. No accounts. You read, you take notes, and you explain firmware. The project runs a small language model on a microcontroller. By Friday you can explain that idea in plain words.

## Part 1: Read and take notes (about 3-4 hours)

Read these in order:

1. What firmware is: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firmware (first 3 sections)
2. What an ESP32 is: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESP32 (first 4 sections)
3. The project readme: https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai (read the description, skip the code)

Take notes as you read, at least 40 lines. Handwritten or typed, your choice. Write what each thing means in your own words. Do not copy-paste.

## Part 2: Your deliverable, firmware 101 (about 2-3 hours)

Write FIRMWARE_101.md.

1. Explain firmware in your own words. How is it different from a program on a laptop? Answer in 5-7 sentences.
2. Draw the boot flow, on paper or in the file: power on, bootloader, firmware starts, firmware reads the model from flash, text comes out. One line per stage.
3. Explain what flashing means. Why does a chip need flashing when a laptop just runs an installer? Answer in 3-5 sentences.
4. Take a photo of the paper drawing if you drew it by hand, and add it to the file.

Rules: the boot flow must make sense to someone who never saw a chip. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.

## Part 3: Meetup teach-back

- 5 minutes: walk the group through your boot flow.
- The lead asks you 2 questions about what you wrote.
