## Phase 1: Reading (about 2-3 hours)

Read these in order:

1. What firmware is: https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/latest/esp32s3/ (read the introduction and the section on the build system)
2. The board overview: https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/latest/esp32s3/api-guides/ (read the names of the sections)
3. The project readme: https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai (read the description, skip the code)

For each source, write in your log:
- what it is, in one line
- 3 things you learned
- 1 question you still have

Write in your own words. Do not copy-paste.

## Phase 2: Coding, in the Wokwi simulator (about 1-2 hours)

Work in the browser, no installs:

1. Open https://wokwi.com. Start a new ESP32 project, the blinking LED example. Press Play. Take a screenshot and save it as BEFORE.png.
2. Open the code editor. Find the delay call. Change it from 1000 ms to 200 ms.
3. Press Play again. Take a screenshot and save it as AFTER.png.
4. Write 3 sentences in your own words: what changed in the code, what flashing means on a real board, and what the simulator skips.

## Phase 3: Doc generation, FIRMWARE_EDIT.md (about 1 hour)

Build FIRMWARE_EDIT.md with these parts:

- BEFORE.png and AFTER.png, embedded side by side.
- The 3 sentences from phase 2.
- One extra sentence: the blink frequency in Hz for both delays, 1000 ms and 200 ms. Frequency = 1 divided by the delay in seconds.

Push FIRMWARE_EDIT.md and both PNGs to this repo.
