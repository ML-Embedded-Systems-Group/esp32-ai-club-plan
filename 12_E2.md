## Part 2: Your deliverable, firmware 101 (about 2-3 hours)

Write FIRMWARE_101.md.

1. Explain firmware in your own words. How is it different from a program on a laptop? Answer in 5-7 sentences.
2. Draw the boot flow, on paper or in the file: power on, bootloader, firmware starts, firmware reads the model from flash, text comes out. One line per stage.
3. Explain what flashing means. Why does a chip need flashing when a laptop just runs an installer? Answer in 3-5 sentences.
4. Take a photo of the paper drawing if you drew it by hand, and add it to the file.

Rules: the boot flow must make sense to someone who never saw a chip. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.
