## Paper details

- **Year:** 2017
- **Source:** [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)

## Why it matters

The Transformer replaces recurrent sequence processing with attention-based layers that can relate positions in a sequence while offering more parallel computation during training. This paper introduced the architecture that became a foundation for modern language and multimodal models.

It is the final pure-ML paper in the Week 2 sequence because it brings together representations, optimization, sequence modeling, and efficient computation. Read it for the design argument and evidence, not just for the familiar architecture diagram.

## Focus questions

- Why do the authors argue that attention can replace recurrence and convolution for this task?
- How do multi-head attention, positional encoding, residual connections, and feed-forward layers fit together?
- What do the translation results, ablations, and training-cost comparisons actually demonstrate?

## Suggested reading

Read the Introduction, Model Architecture, attention, positional encoding, and Results sections. Start with Figures 1 and 2, then inspect the main results and ablation tables to connect the architecture choices to the evidence.
