## Paper details

- **Year:** 2021
- **Source:** [TinyML Platforms Benchmarking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.01319)

## Why it matters

TinyML claims are meaningful only when the hardware, model, and measurement conditions are clear. This paper compares embedded platforms using a common benchmarking approach, helping readers reason about the trade-off between accuracy, speed, memory, energy, and platform capability.

It matters because it turns a collection of device-specific results into a more comparable view of the TinyML landscape. Use it to practice separating a benchmark's measured evidence from a broad claim about the best platform.

## Focus questions

- What does the benchmark measure, and how are workloads and platforms made comparable?
- Which hardware or software factors explain the observed differences?
- What does the benchmark show well, and what does it leave out?

## Suggested reading

Read the abstract, benchmark methodology, platform and workload descriptions, and results discussion. Focus on the platform-comparison tables, performance plots, and any notes about measurement conditions or limitations.
