## Coverage for your notes and report

Use the guide prompts below to understand the paper, then turn the notes into a concise report. Do not copy the abstract or sentences from the paper.

- **Problem / state of the art:** What problem does the paper address, and what was missing or unsatisfactory in existing work?
- **Goal:** What does the paper set out to achieve or demonstrate?
- **Challenges:** What makes the problem difficult, especially under the paper's constraints?
- **Key mechanism:** What is the central method, system, algorithm, or insight? Explain the moving parts that matter.
- **Key results / evidence:** What experiments, tables, figures, measurements, or analysis support the claims?
- **Strengths and improvements:** What is convincing or useful, and what limitation, trade-off, or improvement can you identify?
- **What I learned or liked:** Which idea changed your understanding or was especially clear, surprising, or useful?
- **Summary:** In a few sentences, state the paper's contribution and the evidence behind it.

## What to submit

Submit only one Markdown report at `submissions/week-2/<paper>_<member>/`. Include the paper title, authors, and source link. The report body must be 400–600 words; headings, metadata, and the source link do not count toward that limit.

Do **not** submit code, notebooks, model files, plots, benchmark output, or downloaded papers. Week 2 does not ask you to implement, train, reproduce, or benchmark anything.

```markdown
## Key Mechanism
## Key Results

## What I Learned or Liked
## Summary
```
