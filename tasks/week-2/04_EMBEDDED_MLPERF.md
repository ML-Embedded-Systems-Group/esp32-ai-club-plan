## Paper details

- **Year:** 2021
- **Source:** [MLPerf Tiny Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07597)

## Why it matters

MLPerf Tiny defines shared workloads and rules for evaluating machine learning on tiny, resource-constrained devices. A common benchmark makes results easier to compare and gives researchers and engineers a vocabulary for latency, energy, accuracy, and system constraints.

The paper is useful for learning that a benchmark is a designed measurement system, not just a list of numbers. Its task definitions, quality targets, and reporting rules shape which conclusions the results can support.

## Focus questions

- Why were these workloads and quality metrics selected for TinyML?
- How do the benchmark rules balance accuracy, latency, energy, and fairness?
- Which conclusions are supported by the results, and which would require other workloads or hardware?

## Suggested reading

Read the Introduction, benchmark-workload and methodology sections, rules, and results. Examine the workload diagrams and result tables or figures, especially where accuracy and system metrics are reported together.
