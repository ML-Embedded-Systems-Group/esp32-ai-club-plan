# Phase 2: Maths

**Estimated time: 2 hours**

For every problem, show **every step** of the calculation.

Use:

**Bandwidth = bus width in bytes × transfers per second**

Since:

**bus width in bytes = bus width in bits ÷ 8**

and DDR performs two transfers per clock:

**transfers per second = clock frequency × 2**

Therefore:

**Bandwidth = (bus bits ÷ 8) × clock frequency × 2**

Be careful to distinguish between **MHz**, **MT/s**, and **bytes/s**.
