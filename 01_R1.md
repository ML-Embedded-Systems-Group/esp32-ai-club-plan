## Phase 2: Solve the Maths (2 hours)

Use this joint table:

- `P(A1, B1) = 0.2`
- `P(A1, B2) = 0.3`
- `P(A2, B1) = 0.4`
- `P(A2, B2) = 0.1`

1. Calculate `P(B1)`.
2. Calculate `P(A1 given B1)` with Bayes rule.
3. Show every step.
4. State the expected values: `P(B1) = 0.6` and `P(A1 given B1) = 0.333`.
5. Explain why the frequency of heads approaches `0.5` after many fair coin tosses.

5. Print the frequency and error at each size.
