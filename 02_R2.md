## Phase 2: Maths (about 2 hours)

Initial vectors, dimension 2:

- cat = [0.5, -0.3]
- sat = [-0.4, 0.2]
- dog = [0.6, 0.4]

Problem 1: for center word cat, compute the softmax over all 3 words. Show every step: the three dot products, the exponentials, the sum, the three probabilities.

Verify: the three probabilities sum to 1, and each rounds to the first forward pass of your script to 3 decimals.

Problem 2: cat and dog never appear next to each other. cat and sat do. Think about which words share contexts. Write down your prediction before you run the script: after training, which pair will be most similar, cat-dog or cat-sat?

Verify: your script's cosine table answers this. Your reasoning must match the result.
