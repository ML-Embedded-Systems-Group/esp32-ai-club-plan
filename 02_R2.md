## Phase 3: Maths, units and capacity (about 2 hours)

Show every step, with units:

1. 14.9 MB in bits: 14.9 times 8 times 1,000,000.
2. How many 4-bit values fit: total bits divided by 4.
3. 2 to the power 15 is 32768. The repo vocab is 32768. Why are 15 bits enough for one vocab index?
4. Bytes per vocab index at 15 bits: 15 divided by 8.
5. Free flash: 16 MB minus 14.9 MB. How many 4-bit values fit in the free space?
6. Write 2 sentences: what limits the vocab size, and what the free space is good for.
