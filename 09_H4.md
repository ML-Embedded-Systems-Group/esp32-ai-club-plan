## Part 2: Your deliverable, what tok/s means (about 2-3 hours)

Write WHAT_IS_TOKS.md.

1. Explain tok/s in your own words: tokens generated per second.
2. Do the arithmetic by hand: the chip runs at 9.88 tok/s. How many milliseconds does one token take?
3. The results page says a flash row read takes 20.3 us and the model reads 6 rows per token. Do the arithmetic: total row read time per token in us? Is that a large or small part of one token time?
4. Answer in writing: compare 9.88 tok/s with a laptop. Why is this number fine for a chip and slow for a laptop? What is the chip actually waiting for?

Rules: show every step of the arithmetic. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.
