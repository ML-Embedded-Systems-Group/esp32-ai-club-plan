## Phase 2: Coding, SPEED_SIM.py (about 1-2 hours)

Write a Python script with these requirements:

- Under 30 lines. No libraries beyond time and math.
- Simulate 9.88 tok/s: print 20 tokens, one every 101 milliseconds. Use time.sleep(0.101).
- Time the whole run with time.perf_counter. Print the measured time in seconds and the measured tok/s: 20 divided by the time.
- Print the row-read math: 6 rows times 20.3 microseconds.

Save it as SPEED_SIM.py. Run it and keep the output. Write the script yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool.

## Phase 3: Maths, timing (about 1 hour)

Show every step:

1. One token at 9.88 tok/s: 1 divided by 9.88, in seconds. Convert to milliseconds.
2. Row reads: 6 rows times 20.3 us. Convert to milliseconds.
3. Share of one token: row reads divided by the token time, times 100. Round to 2 decimals. Is it a large or small share?
4. Two sentences: what dominates the token time, and why row reads matter.
