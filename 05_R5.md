## Your role

You run the ple arm (seed 1). You own the quantization (PTQ) verification for the whole project.

## Weekly tasks

### Week 1 — PTQ explainer
- Task: Read research/tinystories/quantize_eval.py and src/quantize.py. Write the explainer: group-wise symmetric int4 PTQ, group 64 vs 128, fp16 scales.
- Deliverable: The 1-page PTQ explainer.
- Verify: R2 can answer a quiz from the explainer.
- Learn: Neural network basics. Watch the 3Blue1Brown series on neural networks.

### Week 2 — Launch ple seed 1
- Task: Launch the run: `uv run python -m research.tinystories.train --arm ple --vocab 32768 --steps 3000 --target-core 559000 --seed 1` on your Colab account.
- Deliverable: The run reaches 3000 steps. The checkpoint and the run JSON record land in `runs/`.
- Verify: Checkpoints save to Google Drive every 1000 steps.
- Learn: Embeddings and next-token prediction. Explain why the ple table is 25M parameters.

### Week 3 — PTQ group 64
- Task: Run the group-64 PTQ eval for both seeds: `uv run python -m research.tinystories.quantize_eval --tag cleandeploy --seed 0` and `--seed 1`.
- Deliverable: The PTQ degradation table (fp32 vs 4-bit) for all arms.
- Verify: Your table matches the published shape: ple degrades less than baseline.
- Learn: The transformer block. Explain where the weights of one block live.

### Week 4 — PTQ report
- Task: Write the PTQ report. Does the ple gain survive 4-bit in our runs? The published claim is 124-128% retained.
- Deliverable: The PTQ section of the verification report.
- Verify: Your numbers sit near the published retention range.
- Learn: Perplexity and evaluation. Explain why a big redundant table survives quantization.

### Week 5 — Eval protocol design
- Task: Design the eval protocol for the improvise model: ppl plus a human coherence score.
- Deliverable: The protocol doc.
- Verify: Two members score the same text and land within 1 point.
- Learn: RoPE and KV cache. Draw the cache growth over 200 tokens.

### Week 6 — Quantize the domain model
- Task: Quantize the fine-tuned model with PTQ. Verify the quality stays.
- Deliverable: The 4-bit domain model and its ppl.
- Verify: The ppl delta from fp32 stays near the published delta.
- Learn: Fine-tuning and PTQ. Explain the order: fine-tune first, then quantize.

### Week 7 — Teach LayerNorm
- Task: Present LayerNorm and residual connections to the team at the meetup.
- Deliverable: Your teach-back slides.
- Verify: H4 can explain why residuals help training.
- Learn: Prepare your presentation deeply.

### Week 8 — What I learned
- Task: Write the "what I learned" page. One page, honest.
- Deliverable: MY_LEARNING.md in the club repo.
- Verify: You can teach the page to a new member in 10 minutes.
- Learn: Your own writing is the learning.
