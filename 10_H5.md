## Phase 2: Coding, RUNTIME_SIM.py (about 1-2 hours)

Write a Python script with these requirements:

- Under 50 lines. No libraries beyond time and random. No ML, just fake data.
- Fake table: 100 rows, 8 values each, random. Fake head: 10 values, random.
- 10 steps. Each step: read 6 rows from the table, scan the head, pick the highest value, print the picked index as the token.
- Time each step with time.perf_counter. Print each step time, the average, and the average as tok/s: 1000 divided by the average in ms.

Save it as RUNTIME_SIM.py. Run it and keep the output. Write the script yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool.
