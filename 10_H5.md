## Part 2: Your artifact, the mini runtime simulator (about 2-3 hours)

1. Write a Python script, under 50 lines. No ML, just fake data.
2. Make a fake table: 100 rows, 8 values each, random. A fake head: 10 values, random.
3. Simulate 10 steps. Each step: read 6 rows from the table, scan the head, pick the token with the highest value, print it. Time each step.
4. Print the 10 tokens and the average step time.
5. Save as RUNTIME_SIM.py. Paste the output into RUNTIME_SIM.md. Push both to this repo.

Rules: write the script yourself. No copy-paste. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.
