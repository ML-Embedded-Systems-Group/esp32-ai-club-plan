## Phase 2: Maths, softmax by hand (about 2 hours)

Show every step:

1. Logits: [1, 2, 3]. Softmax at T = 1: e to the power 1, e to the power 2, e to the power 3. Compute the three exponentials, sum them, and the three probabilities. Round to 3 decimals.
2. Verify: the probabilities sum to 1.
3. T goes to 0: the largest logit wins. Show that the probability of the max approaches 1.
4. T = 10: recompute the three probabilities. Which is closer to uniform?
5. Greedy: with probabilities 0.09, 0.24, 0.67, the pick is the index with the largest probability. Which one?
6. Write 2 sentences: what temperature controls, and why greedy always picks the same token.
