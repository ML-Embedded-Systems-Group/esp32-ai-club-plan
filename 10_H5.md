## Phase 2: Coding, RUNTIME.py, the runtime seed (about 2-3 hours)

This is the first pass at the runtime loop the club finishes in week 2. Build it now:

1. Download the real model file: https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai/raw/main/src/model.bin . Save it as model.bin in your working folder.
2. Write a script, under 80 lines, stdlib only (struct, time).
3. Load model.bin: treat the first 10 KB as the head, the rest as the table. Row stride: 512 bytes. The real layout gets fixed in week 2; this pass proves the loop.
4. For the first step: read the 6 rows, print their values, print the chosen index and why (the largest value).
5. 20 steps. Each step: read 6 rows from the table, find the largest value in the 6 rows, use its index as the next token id, print it.
6. Time each step with time.perf_counter. Print each step time, the average, and the tok/s: 1,000,000 divided by the average in us.
7. A comment at the top: what must change in week 2 (the real layout, the real head).
8. Run it twice and keep both outputs.

Save it as RUNTIME.py. Write the script yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool.

## Phase 3: Maths, measured speed (about 2 hours)

Show every step, with units:

1. Your average step time from the script, in milliseconds.
2. Your measured tok/s: 1000 divided by the average in ms.
3. The repo reports 9.88 tok/s. Your speed as a percentage: yours divided by 9.88, times 100.
4. Your loop reads 6 rows of 512 bytes per step. Bytes per step: 6 times 512. How much memory bandwidth does your loop need? Bytes per second: bytes per step times your measured steps per second.
5. Write 2 sentences: why your loop is faster or slower, and which parts the real chip pays for.
