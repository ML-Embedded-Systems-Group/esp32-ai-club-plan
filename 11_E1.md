## Part 1: Read and keep a reading log (about 3-4 hours)

Read these in order:

1. What an ESP32 is: https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/latest/esp32s3/ (read the introduction)
2. The board overview: https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/latest/esp32s3/api-guides/ (read the names of the sections, then the one on memory)
3. The project readme: https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai (read the description, skip the code)

For each source, write in your log:
- what it is, in one line
- 3 things you learned
- 1 question you still have

Write in your own words. Do not copy-paste. Your questions feed the meetup.

## Part 2: Your artifact, run the chip in the browser (about 2-3 hours)

1. Open https://wokwi.com. It simulates real boards in the browser. No account needed to run.
2. Start a new ESP32 project, the blinking LED example.
3. Press Play. Watch the LED blink. Take a screenshot and save it as WOKWI_RUN.png.
4. In the simulator, find the ESP32 chip and the LED. Write 3 sentences: which board parts you see, and where the program and its data live in the sim.
5. Save the screenshot and the 3 sentences as WOKWI_RUN.md. Push it to this repo.

Rules: your 3 sentences must be your own words. No copy-paste. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.

## Part 3: Meetup demo

- 5 minutes: run the simulator live and show the blinking LED.
