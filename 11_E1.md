# E1: Embedded, week 1

Member: ____________
Time budget: 5-8 hours across the week.

## What this week gives you

No code this week. No flashing. No accounts. You read, you take notes, and you explain the board. The project runs a small language model on a microcontroller. By Friday you can explain that idea in plain words.

## Part 1: Read and take notes (about 3-4 hours)

Read these in order:

1. What an ESP32 is: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESP32 (first 4 sections)
2. What flash memory is: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flash_memory (first 3 sections)
3. The project readme: https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai (read the description, skip the code)
4. The results page: https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai/blob/main/RESULTS.md (look at the numbers, skip the code)

Take notes as you read, at least 40 lines. Handwritten or typed, your choice. Write what each thing means in your own words. Do not copy-paste.

## Part 2: Your deliverable, the board (about 2-3 hours)

Write THE_BOARD.md.

1. Explain the ESP32-S3 parts: the CPU (240 MHz, 2 cores), 512 KB SRAM, 8 MB PSRAM, 16 MB flash. One paragraph each.
2. Answer in writing: why does the board need PSRAM when it already has SRAM? What is each one for?
3. The model file is about 14.9 MB. Where does it live and why? Answer in 3-5 sentences.
4. The results page says PSRAM speed is 60.7 MB/s and SRAM speed is 240 MB/s. Which one is faster and why does that matter for the model?

Rules: plain words only. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.

## Part 3: Meetup teach-back

- 5 minutes: explain the board parts to the group.
- The lead asks you 2 questions about what you wrote.
