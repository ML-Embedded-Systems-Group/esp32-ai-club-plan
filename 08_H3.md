## Part 2: Your deliverable, the model file (about 2-3 hours)

Write THE_MODEL_FILE.md.

1. Explain in one paragraph what model.bin is: one file that holds the whole model and the tokenizer.
2. Do the arithmetic by hand:
   - The table of weights is about 25M values at 4 bits each. Size in bytes?
   - The head is about 1.64 MB at 4 bits. At 2 values per byte, how many values is that? Cross-check: vocab 32768 times d_model 96 is about how many?
   - The core is about 273 KB at 4 bits. At 2 values per byte, how many values is that?
   - Add the three sizes. Compare the total with the published model.bin size, about 14.9 MB.
3. Answer in writing: what else is inside the file? (Hint: the tokenizer needs its pieces stored somewhere.)
4. Explain why the model ships as one file instead of many files.

Rules: show every step of the arithmetic. No copy-paste. Write everything yourself. Do not paste from any AI tool. The meetup questions will show the difference.
